      OMFED NEER: Packaged Drinking Water from Odisha

      OMFED NEER is packaged drinking water with added mineral. It carries the OMFED brand: OMFED, The Odisha State Cooperative Milk Producers' Federation Ltd., is the brand owner, as printed on every label. Praayog Associate Private Limited manufactures and markets it.

      The water is processed using reverse osmosis, UV treatment and ozonation (label wording), with added minerals, and sealed in PET bottles of 1 litre, 500 ml and 250 ml. We supply distributors, dealers, shops, offices, institutions and events in Odisha.

      What we promise

      Only what is on the label: water type, treatment, minerals, pack size and how to store it. No claims we can't show.

      How we work

      Through distributors and dealers in each district, with GST invoices and a signed agreement before any payment.

      Brand Owner: OMFED

      The Odisha State Cooperative Milk Producers' Federation Ltd. (OMFED) is Odisha's apex dairy cooperative, founded in 1980 under the Cooperative Societies Act. Its milk and milk products are sold across Odisha under the OMFED brand, and its mascot, Balaram, appears on this site with OMFED NEER.
