      Our Distribution Network in Odisha

      We are building the OMFED NEER network district by district, from our operations office in Bhubaneswar and our manufacturing unit in Jajpur. Enquiries are open in all 30 districts. Pick your district on the map or in the list to start.

      Who We Work With

      Distributors

      Firms with storage and vehicles that supply dealers and shops across an area.

      Dealers & wholesalers

      Businesses that stock OMFED NEER for a market or a town.

      Retailers & shops

      Shops that sell OMFED NEER to families and travellers. We connect you with the distributor for your area.

      Institutional buyers

      Offices, schools, hospitals, hotels, caterers and event organisers who buy in bulk.

      How It Works

      1. Apply

        Send your name, firm, district and phone. No bank, Aadhaar or PAN details at this stage.

      2. Screening

        We review your area, reach and storage, and ask for documents only if we go ahead.

      3. Agreement

        We meet, agree the area and terms, and sign the distributor agreement.

      4. Launch

        First stock arrives with our sales officer, who helps you start in your area.

      We never ask for payment before a signed agreement. Payments go only to Praayog Associate Private Limited's company bank account. If anyone asks you to pay an "application fee" or a deposit into a personal account, it is not us: please tell us on WhatsApp.

      Distributor & Dealer Enquiry

      Send opens WhatsApp (or your email app) with your message ready; you press send. This website does not store what you type. Please don't share bank, Aadhaar or PAN details at this stage.

      Bulk Orders

      For offices, schools, hospitals, hotels, events and government buyers. Every supply comes with a GST tax invoice.

      1. Tell us

        Organisation, district, pack size and the monthly or one-time quantity.

      2. Get a quote

        Our sales team replies with the price and the delivery plan.

      3. Delivery

        Delivered with a dispatch letter and a GST invoice.
