      Quality & Safety

      Home > Quality & Safety

      What's in the Bottle

      OMFED NEER is packaged drinking water with added mineral. It is not natural mineral water. The label lists the ingredients as treated water and minerals, with these added mineral salts per 100 ml:

      Salts of calcium chloride1.0 mg
      Magnesium sulphate0.5 mg
      Potassium bicarbonate0.1 mg

      Source: nutritional information on the printed label (quantity per 100 ml).

      How It's Treated

      The label states that OMFED NEER is processed using reverse osmosis, UV treatment and ozonation. In plain words:

      1. Reverse osmosis

        Water is pushed through a very fine membrane that holds back most dissolved salts and particles.

      2. UV treatment

        The water passes under ultraviolet light, a standard step in treating drinking water.

      3. Ozonation

        Ozone is added to the treated water before it is bottled, another standard treatment step.

      Packing & Hygiene

      Sealed bottle

      Every bottle is capped and sealed. The label says: do not buy if the seal is broken or tampered with.

      Batch and date

      The batch number, manufacturing date and MRP are printed on the cap or neck, so each bottle can be traced to its batch.

      Packed by the box

      1 litre in boxes of 12, 500 ml in boxes of 24 and 250 ml in boxes of 48, delivered with an invoice.

      Recyclable PET

      The bottle is recyclable PET. Please crush it after use and don't reuse it for anything else.

      Storage & Use

      • Store under cool, dry and hygienic conditions.
      • Keep the bottle away from direct sunlight.
      • Best before 6 months from the date of manufacture.
      • Do not buy if the seal is broken or tampered with.
      Something not right? Tell us the batch number from the cap or neck, where you bought it and what you noticed. Message us on WhatsApp or email customercare@praayogassociate.com.
